# Multiplayer

Blackwall Runner should support multiple persistent players sharing one authoritative world.

Future multiplayer responsibilities include:

- Player identity
- Persistent characters
- Parties and leaders
- Following another player
- Split-party play
- Absent-player handling
- Rejoining the active party
- Action conflict resolution
- Concurrent updates
- Player-specific information
- Shared world notifications

The engine, not an individual interface, must own multiplayer truth.
