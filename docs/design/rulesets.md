# Rulesets

Rulesets define mechanical behaviour independently from setting lore.

A ruleset may define:

- Attributes
- Skills
- Resolution mechanics
- Combat
- Damage
- Armour
- Movement
- Healing
- Equipment behaviour
- Character creation
- Advancement

The initial planned ruleset families are:

- Near-future
- Fantasy
- Space opera

Ruleset packages should be versioned and loaded through campaign configuration.

Commercial rulesets must use original or appropriately licensed material.
