# Content Safety

AI-generated content should follow a closed-world model.

The AI may use:

- Approved campaign data
- Approved setting terminology
- Original generated content
- Licensed material explicitly included by the selected package

The AI should not introduce external fictional canon, characters, organisations, locations or products.

## Future safeguards

- Setting-specific allowlists
- Prohibited-name lists
- Entity validation
- Similarity checks
- Content provenance
- Output scanning
- Repair or regeneration of invalid narration
- Commercial legal review

Prompt instructions alone are not sufficient protection. Validation should also occur in engine-controlled systems.
